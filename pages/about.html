Bio

I am Muhammad Adnan Shahzad, a machine learning and AI researcher and engineer. My work sits at the intersection of physics-informed machine learning, generative AI, computer vision, and deployment / MLOps. I build and publish open-source research code alongside my papers, with reproducible pipelines end-to-end.

I work primarily in C++, Python, and Java in Unix/Linux environments, using (X)Emacs, Vim, and gVim as IDEs, and tools like PyTorch, PennyLane, PyCBC, GWpy, and LALSuite for research.
