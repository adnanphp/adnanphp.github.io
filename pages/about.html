Bio

Interdisciplinary researcher with a PhD in Physics (University of Camerino, Italy, 2015) and current training in Computer Science (Concordia University, Montreal, expected 2026). My work focuses on Machine Learning, Generative Models, and Data-Driven Systems, with a background in stochastic processes, numerical simulation, and optimization.

Hands-on experience in deep learning, computer vision, large-scale ML pipelines, and end-to-end MLOps — from data ingestion and model training to containerized deployment, monitoring, and CI/CD. I build and publish open-source research code alongside my papers, with reproducible pipelines end-to-end.

I work primarily in Python, C++, and Java in Unix/Linux environments, using (X)Emacs, Vim, and gVim as IDEs, and tools like PyTorch, PennyLane, PyCBC, GWpy, LALSuite, Hugging Face, and LangGraph for research.
