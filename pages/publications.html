  • Deep Learning Detection of Beyond-General-Relativity Deviations in Gravitational-Wave Signals: A Detection-Threshold Study with Real LIGO Noise

    Muhammad Adnan Shahzad

    arXiv:2609.19416 [gr-qc] · Submitted 16 Sep 2026

    A hybrid CNN + hand-crafted statistics classifier is trained to distinguish General-Relativity gravitational-wave waveforms from three families of beyond-GR deviations (amplitude, phase, frequency modulation), using both synthetic aLIGO-PSD noise and real LIGO H1 strain. Using GW150914 as a template, we measure a detection threshold at β ≈ 0.25 with accuracy rising smoothly from chance at β ≤ 0.2 to perfect classification at β ≥ 0.5.

  • Hybrid Quantum-Classical Model for Image Classification

    Muhammad Adnan Shahzad

    arXiv:2509.13353 [cs.CV] · Submitted 14 Sep 2025

    A systematic comparison between hybrid quantum-classical neural networks and purely classical CNNs across MNIST, CIFAR100, and STL10. Hybrid models achieve higher accuracy — 99.38%, 41.69%, and 74.05% respectively — while training 5–12× faster with 6–32% fewer parameters. Adversarial robustness is tested at ε = 0.1.
