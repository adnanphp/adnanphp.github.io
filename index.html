Welcome

I am Muhammad Adnan Shahzad — an interdisciplinary researcher with a PhD in Physics and current training in Computer Science (Concordia University, Montreal).

My work spans Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, Generative AI, Data Analytics & BI, and MLOps — from building end-to-end ML pipelines and REST APIs to deploying containerized models on Kubernetes with full observability.
