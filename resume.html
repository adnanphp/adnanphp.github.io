Deep Learning Detection of Beyond-GR Deviations in Gravitational Waves (2026)

  • Machine-learning pipeline to detect controlled deviations from General Relativity in gravitational-wave signals using synthetic aLIGO noise and real LIGO H1 detector strain from GWOSC.
  • Hybrid classifier combining a 1D CNN on 16,384-sample waveforms with 10 hand-crafted statistics; total 31,697 parameters.
  • Measured detectability curve using real GW150914 strain; detection threshold at β ≈ 0.25 with accuracy rising from chance (β ≤ 0.2) to perfect classification (β ≥ 0.5).
  • Generalization to unseen deviation types (88–100% accuracy) and binary mass ratios q = 1 to q = 10 (89–93%).
  • Stack: PyCBC, LALSuite, GWpy, PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib, LaTeX.
